This update adds the ability to play Pzordles of the past as well as 6 new animals



<b>Added:</b>
- Added a dropdown to select a date of the past so you can replay or play a day that you missed.

<b>New Animals:</b>

- Added Common Rudd
- Added Lehmann's Poison Frog
- Added Menelaus Blue Morpho
- Added Nyala
- Added Prezwalski's Horse
- Added White-backed Vulture
